# Cinematic water SFX licenses

The six files in this directory are modified exports of **6 Short water splashes**.

- Original author: ezwa
- Cut/submitted by: qubodup
- Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/6-short-water-splashes
- Original recording references:
  - http://www.pdsounds.org/sounds/emptying_syringe_in_water_slow
  - http://www.pdsounds.org/sounds/emptying_syringe_in_water_fast
- License: CC0 1.0 / public-domain dedication (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Attribution requirement: none. This voluntary credit is retained for provenance.
- Modifications: leading/trailing silence trim, gentle high/low-pass cleanup,
  fixed peak gain, a safety limiter, 4 ms click-prevention fade-in, downmix to
  mono, resample to 48 kHz, and Ogg Vorbis encoding. No pitch shifting, time
  stretching, synthetic layering, denoising, saturation, or aggressive dynamics
  compression was applied.

The processed files remain usable for commercial and non-commercial projects
